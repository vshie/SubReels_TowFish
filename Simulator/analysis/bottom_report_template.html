Towfish Bottom Following

Does SURF TRACK keep the towfish 4 m above the seabed? Tested on a 6 → 3 → 6 m slope and over a coral head, on the 8 m and 25 m Kevlar tethers. The vertical rates come from the 4 Nov logs and the sim. Every run tows a straight line at 1 m/s through ArduSub SITL and the extension's own code.

Coral head, 3 m highYes, it dodges

As shipped now: clear over the top. Without action the fish would clear it by about 1.2 m. It starts climbing 6 m before the head on the 8 m tether and 22 m before on the 25 m.

Slope 6 → 3 → 6 mFollows it and comes back down

Median altitude error where 4 m is possible. Over the 3 m patch it drives at about 0.6 m. Before the fixes it rode 0.55 m shallow and never got back to 2 m.

RollLevel throughout

Peak roll under 5.1° in every terrain run, no auto-righting rescues. Straight lines only; turns were the earlier studies.

Vertical rates

Measured, not assumed. Sim runs fly scripted stick steps in ALT_HOLD on the Kevlar tether with the weight under the tube. They show that ALT_HOLD's pilot-speed cap sets the rate. Boat speed (0.8–1.2 m/s) changes it by under 0.04 m/s. Negative is climb; m/s.

4 Nov fish log, ALT_HOLD at about 1 m/s with caps 25/20: clean full-up climbs 0.16–0.25 m/s, full-down dives 0.04–0.15 m/s, matching the sim. The physical limit sits above the caps: dives at 0.2–0.49 m/s in STABILIZE, and ALT_HOLD at the 60/40 caps climbed about 0.6 m/s. The sim's STABILIZE climbs measured nothing, because the fish had floated to the surface before the up steps.

Every terrain run

Clearance is seabed minus fish depth at the fish's centre of gravity. Repeat runs with a different Ping packet schedule agreed to 0.02 m, so most variants ran once.

Assumptions and limits

  • Straight tows at 1 m/s in still water. The slope and coral head sit on the track; a head off to one side is seen later or not at all.
  • Ping beam 30° (datasheet), first return, transducer at the boat's tow point. In the model the beam sees the head about 2 m before the boat is over its base.
  • Ping timing and dropouts replay the 4 Nov log packet by packet; good readings get 5 mm of noise.
  • Tow delay comes from the extension's drag position model with the tether length set. With the tether length at 0, the old fixed offset is used.
  • Clearance is measured at the fish's CG. The tow bar and wings extend about 0.15 m below it.
